# Optimized Prompt

Act as an enterprise-architecture discovery specialist.

Prepare a regulation-blind interview to extract the operational footprint of an Infrastructure Engineer who also serves as a Cloud Database Administrator at a DORA-regulated financial entity.

Focus on their work across internal infrastructure, the cloud platform, and production databases, including routine administration, changes, and production/on-call responsibility.

## Requirements

- Do not mention DORA, compliance, regulation, audit, ArchiMate, or architecture modelling.
- Use short, open-ended, non-leading questions.
- Cover core responsibilities, workflows, systems and data, internal and external dependencies, failures and recovery, approvals, ownership, and incident escalation.
- Elicit concrete actor-action-target facts for every named system, activity, dependency, approval, or output.
- Include a response line after every question.
- Do not invent systems, controls, responsibilities, or organizational facts.

## Output Format

Output a six-part interview guide with questions, nudges, fallback questions, and alternative angles.
