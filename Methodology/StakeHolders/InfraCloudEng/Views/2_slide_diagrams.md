# DORA Stakeholder View Slide Diagrams

This deck is the approved presentation-layer companion to the formal viewpoint graph and narrative. Each slide preserves its formal view's complete granular-control breakdown and relationship inventory. The ASCII sketch selects only relationships already approved in the view graph; it never introduces a new relationship or ownership claim.

## Reading Guide

- The relationship inventory is the complete record for each view; a sketch is intentionally condensed for presentation readability.
- A directly performed contribution is shown only where the approved evidence establishes it. Other entries preserve the stated evidence or ownership boundary.
- The overview retains the full DORA legal baseline. Stakeholder-focused slides retain only their approved scoped controls. Operational-context slides add no legal control family.
- Continuity preparedness, recovery and restoration, and operational learning remain separate views.
