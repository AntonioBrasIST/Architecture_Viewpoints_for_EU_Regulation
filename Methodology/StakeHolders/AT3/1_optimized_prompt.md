# Optimized Stakeholder-Regulation Viewpoint Prompt

## Context

- **Stakeholder:** Back-end Developer at a very large Portuguese bank
- **Operational domain:** Internal Enterprise Data Platform
- **Platform context:** An internal platform that makes data available to other services within the bank
- **Target regulation:** Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA)
- **Artifact directory:** `Methodology/StakeHolders/BankBackEndDev_2/`

## Objective

Discover the stakeholder's evidence-backed operational footprint and use it to generate a traceable regulatory architecture viewpoint.

## Tasks

1. Design a regulation-blind stakeholder interview covering daily work, workflows, responsibilities, data and systems, dependencies, approvals, incidents, constraints, and friction.
2. Extract only operational facts explicitly supported by the stakeholder's answers.
3. Classify the stakeholder and identify every distinct concern without consolidating separate concerns.
4. Evaluate DORA only after the operational footprint is approved.
5. Map applicable legal requirements to verified canonical legal-text anchors and operational evidence.
6. Define and materialize the stakeholder-specific ArchiMate viewpoint.
7. Produce the approved narrative, diagrams, selected model representation, reconciliation report, and optional gap-free derivatives.

## Constraints

- Follow the repository's sequential pipeline and every human-validation gate.
- Pause for explicit approval before writing an artifact or advancing to the next stage.
- Do not infer the stakeholder's seniority, technical architecture, responsibilities, processes, dependencies, DORA applicability, compliance status, control maturity, or ownership.
- Mark genuine analytical gaps explicitly and non-judgmentally.
- Preserve traceability between interview evidence, operational facts, concerns, legal requirements, canonical legal-text IDs, coverage records, and model elements.
- Store all generated artifacts only under `Methodology/StakeHolders/BankBackEndDev_2/`.

## Validation

- **Gate:** Confirm Optimized Prompt
- **Status:** Approved by the operator
